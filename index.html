I am a researcher in human-computer interaction and hybrid intelligence, working at the intersection of AI agents and human intelligence. My work spans crowd-powered systems, conversational agents, social robotics, and human-AI interaction, with a current focus on agentic AI and agent-based modelling. I completed my PhD at Eindhoven University of Technology and have held research and teaching positions at TU Delft and Wageningen University & Research. Draft bio — to be refined.
